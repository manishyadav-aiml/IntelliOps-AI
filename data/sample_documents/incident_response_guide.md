# Incident Response Guide

> DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT for IntelliOps AI. Generic process description.

## Lifecycle
Incidents move through OPEN, INVESTIGATING, MITIGATED and RESOLVED. Re-open an incident if symptoms return.

## Roles and communication
Assign one person to lead the investigation. Keep timestamped notes of what was observed, what was tried and what changed. Notes make later reviews and reports reliable.

## Distinguishing findings
Keep these separate in notes and reports: the detected anomaly, observed symptoms, potential contributing factors, suspected root causes, and confirmed root causes. A cause is only confirmed with supporting evidence and human review.

## Severity levels
P1 critical: major outage or data loss risk. P2 high: significant degradation. P3 medium: limited impact. P4 low: minor or informational.

## After resolution
Record resolution notes, verify metrics returned to normal, and capture follow-up actions. Review whether alerts fired early enough.
